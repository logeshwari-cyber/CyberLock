🛡️ Cybersecurity Tips

Best practices to keep your accounts secure

🔴 CRITICAL
📏
Use 12+ Characters
Longer passwords exponentially increase security. Each extra character multiplies the possible combinations.
Example: MySecure#Pass2024!
🔴 CRITICAL
🚫
Never Reuse Passwords
If one account is breached, reused passwords give attackers access to all your accounts.
Use a password manager like Bitwarden or 1Password
🟠 HIGH
📱
Enable MFA/2FA
Multi-factor authentication adds an extra layer. Even if your password is stolen, attackers need your device too.
Use Google Authenticator or Authy
🟠 HIGH
📖
Avoid Dictionary Words
Dictionary attacks can crack common words in seconds. Combine random words or use character substitutions.
❌ sunshine → ✅ $unSh1ne#42!
🟡 MEDIUM
🔔
Monitor Breach Alerts
Sign up for haveibeenpwned.com alerts to know when your email appears in data breaches.
Visit haveibeenpwned.com to check now
🟡 MEDIUM
🔄
Rotate Sensitive Passwords
Change passwords for critical accounts (banking, email) every 3-6 months or after any breach notification.
Set a calendar reminder every 90 days
🟢 GOOD PRACTICE
🧩
Use Passphrases
A random sequence of 4+ words is both strong and memorable. "Purple$Horse*Running!Cloud" is excellent.
Try our Passphrase Generator above!
🟢 GOOD PRACTICE
🌐
Use a VPN on Public WiFi
Public networks can be monitored. A VPN encrypts your traffic, preventing password interception.
ProtonVPN, Mullvad, or NordVPN are recommended

✅ DO

  • Use a unique password for every account
  • Use uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols
  • Use a reputable password manager
  • Enable two-factor authentication everywhere
  • Check if your email was breached
  • Keep your software updated

❌ DON'T

  • Use your name, birthday, or pet's name
  • Use "password", "123456", "qwerty"
  • Share passwords via email or text
  • Write passwords on sticky notes
  • Use the same password for 2+ sites
  • Auto-save passwords in browsers
